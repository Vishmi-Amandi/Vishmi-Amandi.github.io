My Design Process

A user-centered, iterative approach — every project starts with empathy and ends with testing.

  1. 01

    Empathize

    User interviews, surveys, and competitive analysis to understand real needs.

  2. 02

    Define

    Personas, user flows, and problem statements that focus the work.

  3. 03

    Ideate

    Sketches, wireframes, and information architecture.

  4. 04

    Prototype

    High-fidelity mockups and interactive Figma prototypes.

  5. 05

    Test

    Usability testing, feedback loops, and iteration.
